What We Do

One point of contact.
Everything covered.

From finding the right instructor to tracking every renewal date, Live Oak Safety handles the administrative side of workplace safety training so your team can stay focused on the job.

Training Coordination

We locate a qualified instructor for your team's needs and handle scheduling, communication, and logistics — so you're not starting from scratch every renewal cycle.

Certification Management

We organize training records, completion dates, credential documents, and course rosters in one place — by employee or location, however you need it.

Expiration Alerts & Renewals

We monitor upcoming expiration dates and reach out before training becomes overdue — then coordinate the renewal so your records stay current without the scramble.

The Process

How it works

Four straightforward steps from initial conversation to ongoing coverage.

1

Tell us what your team needs

We review your workforce, locations, existing records, and training goals.

2

We coordinate the training

Live Oak Safety locates a qualified provider and organizes scheduling, attendance, and course details.

3

Your employees complete the course

Training is delivered at your workplace or another agreed location by a qualified instructor.

4

We keep everything current

Records are organized, expiration dates are tracked, and renewal reminders go out before the next deadline.
