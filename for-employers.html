What to Expect

Simple from day one.

No complicated onboarding. No long-term contract to sign before we start. Just a conversation, a plan, and one contact handling the details going forward.

How we get started

When you reach out, we begin with a brief conversation about your team — how many employees you have, what training you've already completed, what certifications may be coming due, and what your business requires going forward. If you're not sure what's expiring or what you need, that's fine — figuring that out together is part of what we do.

From there, we'll walk you through what we can coordinate and how we'll keep your records organized. Once we have a picture of your situation, we get to work: finding the right instructor, scheduling the session, and setting up your records so nothing falls through the cracks. After training is complete, we're still in your corner — tracking expiration dates and reaching out before renewals come due so you don't have to watch the calendar yourself.

Certification Management

Your records. Organized.

After training is complete, Live Oak Safety helps organize and maintain everything your business needs to stay on top of employee certifications.

What we track and maintain

We keep your certification records organized by employee and location, so you always know where things stand — whether you're preparing for an inspection, onboarding new staff, or just need to know who's up for renewal.

  • Employee training records
  • Completion and expiration dates
  • Certificate and credential documents
  • Course rosters and attendance
  • Training history by employee or location
  • Upcoming renewal requirements

Why it matters

Misplaced certificates, missed expirations, and inconsistent records create real problems — for audits, insurance, liability, and employee safety. We keep it organized so that when someone asks who's certified and when they need to renew, you have the answer.

Whether you have five employees or fifty, across one location or several, the goal is the same: your records are always current and you're never caught off guard by a lapsed certification.

Expiration Alerts & Renewals

We remind you before
it becomes a problem.

Live Oak Safety monitors upcoming expiration dates and contacts your business before training becomes overdue — then handles the coordination from there.

What happens when a renewal is coming up

  • We flag upcoming expirations before they lapse
  • We identify the affected employees
  • We recommend the appropriate course
  • We coordinate an available instructor
  • We schedule the on-site session
  • We update your records after completion

Stay ahead of the calendar

Certification renewals can sneak up fast — especially across a team with different courses expiring at different times. Our job is to catch that before you do, reach out with enough lead time to schedule comfortably, and make the renewal as easy as the original training.

You won't need to keep a spreadsheet, set a calendar reminder, or track down a new instructor from scratch. That's what we're here for.
