Live Oak Safety connects qualified instructors with Central Texas businesses that need certified workplace training. We manage client coordination, scheduling, and follow-up — so you can focus on what you do best.

What to Expect

A straightforward partnership.

No complicated agreements up front. Just a conversation about what you offer, who you are, and how we can send the right work your way.

How the partnership works

When you reach out, we'll want to learn about your background — the programs you're certified to deliver, the credentials you hold, where you're located, and the types of businesses you're comfortable working with. We're building a network of qualified providers across Central Texas so we can match the right instructor to the right client every time.

Once we have a sense of what you offer, we keep you in mind as businesses with relevant needs come to us. When there's a good fit, we'll reach out to coordinate the details — client information, scheduling, location, and any specifics about the group. You show up, deliver the training, and we handle the paperwork, record-keeping, and client follow-up from there. Renewals come back around on a schedule, which means the relationship doesn't end after one session.

Why Partner With Us

Our Provider Network

Who we work with

We partner with instructors and training organizations who are qualified, reliable, and set up to deliver on-site training to employer groups throughout Central Texas.

  • Certified and qualified in your area of training

    We work with instructors who hold the appropriate certifications, credentials, or authorizations for the programs they deliver — whatever the course requires.

  • Available for on-site delivery in Central Texas

    Our clients need training at their location — a job site, office, gym floor, or kitchen. Providers in our network are set up to bring the course to the client, not the other way around.

  • Comfortable working with employer groups

    Our clients are businesses coordinating training for their employees — not individuals signing up for a class. We need providers who are set up for group delivery and can adapt to different workplace environments.

  • Reliable and professional

    Our clients are counting on training to happen when it's scheduled. We work with providers who communicate clearly, show up prepared, and follow through — because our reputation with clients depends on yours.
